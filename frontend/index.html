Our Journey

Year 2024

Eternum unveiled the pioneering interactive digital memory creation platform to revolutionize preservation techniques. This innovative platform not only preserves memories but also serves as a social network, opening up new realms of experiences and opportunities in virtual reality (VR).

Year 2023

Following extensive research, investigation, and field studies, the product was carefully curated and development commenced. The progress in artificial intelligence has enabled possibilities that were previously considered unimaginable just a few years ago.

Year 2022

The team at Eternum was formed through a shared purpose, uniting individuals with diverse expertise including technology innovators, psychologists, designers, entrepreneurs, as well as medical and philosophical professionals.

Year 2021

The passing of a cherished family member of one of Eternum’s founders inspired the drive for innovation in memory conservation, shaping the future of emotional bonds for future generations.

Who are we?

The Eternum team has been developed, funded, and driven by various collaborators committed to ensuring its long-term success. Among them are experts in technology, psychologists, designers, entrepreneurs, doctors, philosophers, artists, and Space Computing engineers. The project has been developing with its own momentum and is getting closer every day…
